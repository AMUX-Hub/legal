When you write, include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The app version (in the app: Settings → Help & about → Build info)
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • Screenshots if you have them

Common questions

  • Restore a subscription on a new device: in the app, Settings → Membership → Restore purchases.
  • Bought AMUX Pro on the web (Whop)? Settings → Membership → Web purchase → Link.
  • Cancel a subscription: Apple purchases are managed in iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions. Whop purchases are managed at whop.com.
  • Export or delete your data: Settings → Privacy & data.
  • Apple Health not connected? Settings → Integrations → Apple Health, or iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → AMUX Pulse.
